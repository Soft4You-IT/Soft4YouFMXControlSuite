# Changelog

The changes of each release of the Soft4You FMX Control Suite, the newest first. The version numbers are explained in the section "Versioning" of the [README](README.md); each release has the sections that apply among **Added**, **Changed**, **Fixed** and **Breaking changes** (changes that can stop existing projects from compiling or forms from loading).

## Unreleased

### Added

- `TS4YStepProgressBar`: progress indicator made of steps along a horizontal or vertical line, with completed, current, pending and failed steps, an animated line that can also show the progress inside the current step, steps that the user can select with the mouse and the keyboard, with a halo around the step under the mouse pointer, and a look that comes from a style, where the markers and the line can have any shape and whose parts are seen and changed in the style designer. The size of the markers, also different for each state, their position on the line or beside it, the spaces between markers, line and labels, and the visibility of the line can be set. The list of the steps has always a first and a last step, and the steps are edited in the IDE with an editor of their own, opened also with a double click on the control. Each step can have a look of its own in the states chosen, with its marker settings, a part of the style for the marker and a text of the style for the caption; the first and the last step of a new control have a start and an end marker.
- `TS4YLinearGauge`: gauge drawn as a horizontal or vertical bar that fills with a value in a range, with a caption and the value written on one side of the bar. A large change of the value is animated, the value has a format of its own or a text given by an event, and the look comes from a style, where the track and the fill can have any shape; properties can replace their brushes. The parts of the styles of the linear and circular gauges and of the meters are seen and changed in the style designer.
- `TS4YCircularGauge`: gauge drawn as a ring, an open arc or a half circle that fills with a value in a range, with the value and the caption in the middle. The start, the length and the direction of the arc can be set, so that it can be drawn in any position, also upside down or on a side; it has the same range, animation, format of the value, brushes and style mechanism as `TS4YLinearGauge`, and the arcs of the style give the brush and the round or flat ends.
- `TS4YLinearMeter` and `TS4YCircularMeter`: the linear and the circular gauge with a graduation, like a ruler or a thermometer and like the dial of an instrument. The graduation has major ticks with their numbers and minor ticks between them, on either side of the bar or inside or outside the arc, with an automatic or fixed step, a format of the numbers, and ticks and numbers whose look comes from the style. Ranges of values with their colors are drawn on the track, in thin bands on its sides or in both places, the fill can take the color of the range of the value, and a target value can be marked across the track. The circular meter shows the value with a needle, with the texts beside its center on the side opposite to the arc and the fill hidden by default; the linear meter can add a pointer beside the bar and hide the fill.
- `TS4YSkinCircularGauge`: circular gauge whose look is made only of the parts of its style, such as a dial and a needle drawn as images or shapes, resized together with the gauge. The gauge turns the needle of the style to the value, with the angles and the center of rotation of a hidden arc of the style or with its own angles, and writes the value and the caption in the texts of the style; it has the same range, animation and format of the value as the other gauges. The collection `Needles` turns other needles of the style to values of their own, with their own range, scale and wrap around the scale, for the hands of a clock or a small dial inside the main one.
- `TS4YSkinLinearGauge`: linear gauge whose look is made only of the parts of its style, such as a thermometer or a level drawn as images or shapes. The gauge moves a pointer of the style along a scale of the style, horizontal or vertical and from either end, uncovers the filled part of an image of the style and writes the value and the caption in the texts of the style. The collection `Pointers` moves other pointers of the style to values of their own.
- `TS4YPieChart`: pie chart, or ring chart with a hole in the middle, with a legend on any side, values, percents or captions written inside or beside the slices, the total in the middle of the ring, an animation when the chart appears and when its data change, and a click on a slice. The slices are stored in the form or filled by the LiveBindings through a standard `TLinkGridToDataSource`, kept apart from the stored ones, with the current record following the current slice and an event to change the look of each bound slice. The colors come from a palette, of the chart or of the style, and the look from a style, whose parts are seen and changed in the style designer, as in the styles of the bar and line charts.
- `TS4YBarChart`: bar chart, with vertical or horizontal bars, categories and series side by side, a value axis with an automatic or fixed range and grid lines, grid lines between the categories, values written beyond the bars, negative values, a legend of the series, animation and a click on a bar. Like the pie chart, the data are stored in the form or filled by a `TLinkGridToDataSource`, where each column after the first is a series, with events to change the look of the bound series and categories.
- `TS4YLineChart`: line chart, with the same categories, series, axes and LiveBindings as the bar chart, a line for each series with points on the values, and the area under the lines that can be filled with the color of the series.
- `TS4YGrid`: grid (a `TGrid`) with a filter row under the header, where the user writes for each column a filter made of an operator and a value (`abc`, `= abc`, `! abc`, `abc*`, `*abc`, `> 10`, `<= 15/03/2026`, `=` for the empty values, `<>` for the others, double quotes for a text as it is), read with the type of the column: text, number, date, time, a value of a list (a `TPopupColumn` or the values given in `FilterItems`, also as text and value, such as `Open=1`, filled from an enumerated type with `SetFilterEnum`, for columns whose cells show a text for each value); a check column has a check box with three states. A text that cannot be read takes a color of the style, and Esc clears a filter. The button of each field opens a menu with the operators, the values of the list, a calendar, today or the current time and an item that clears the filter, which the application can change with `OnFilterMenu` or replace with a `TPopupMenu` of its own; the style of the popup, of its items and of its calendar can be chosen. The filters apply only while the filter row is shown, which the user shows and hides with a button on the right side of the header or with Ctrl+Shift+F, and the application with `ToggleFilterRow`; the settings of the grid and the public properties `Filtered` and `Sorted` can be bound with the LiveBindings, which are told when they change. The grid fires `OnFilter` when the application must filter its data, with Enter or by itself after a pause in the typing with `AutoFilter`, and gives the operator and the value of each filter, for a query, or compares the values of the rows with `FilterMatches`, for the data in memory. A footer can show under each column the sum, the average, the count, the lowest or the highest value of the rows of the grid, or a text, with the format of the column or one of its own, and `OnGetFooterText` to change it. The filter row and the footer are added to the style of the grid, from the platform or from a style book, which can give them a background, the font of the footer and the space around the editors and the texts, and follow the width, the order, the visibility and the horizontal scroll of the columns. A style `S4YGridStyle` in the style book is the style of all the grids of the form, and Edit Custom Style copies the parts of the filter row and of the footer too, ready to be changed. A click on the header sorts the rows by a column, Shift+click by more columns, Ctrl+click removes a column and Alt+click goes back to the order of the data; the header shows the arrow of the direction and the position of each key, and the application sorts its data in `OnSort`. The height of the header can come from the style, from `HeaderHeight` or from the height of the rows, which it then follows when `RowHeight` changes.

### Fixed

- `TS4YBadge` and `TS4YExpander` use a style named `S4YBadgeStyle` or `S4YExpanderStyle` in the style book of the form or in the active style, instead of always the style linked into the package. A `StyleLookup` that names no style makes them use their own style: before, they were left without one.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-29

First public release.

### Added

- `TS4YHTMLEditor`: WYSIWYG HTML editor with formatting, lists, tables, images, links, placeholders, find and replace.
- `TS4YFlexBox` and `TS4YFlexItem`: container that lays out its items with the CSS Flexbox model (W3C Level 1).
- `TS4YBlockScrollBox`: scroll box that stacks its children as the blocks of the CSS flow; a `TS4YFlexBox` in it behaves as a block of a web page.
- `TS4YBadge`: badge like the notification counter of a smartphone icon, with a number or a short text.
- `TS4YExpander`: collapsible panel with an icon in the header, a height animation, a click on the whole header and a badge counter.
- `TS4YColorSelector`: foreground and background color swatches, like in the drawing programs.
- Runtime and design-time packages for Delphi 11, 12 and 13, also for the 64-bit IDE of Delphi 12.3 and newer.
- The IDE splash screen and About box show the version of the installed suite.
